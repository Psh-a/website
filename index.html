Chris Wan

All of my friends call me Crispy, because it rhymes with my preferred name, Chris.

Quite coincidentally, it sums up my personality. Much like a bag of crisps, I like to finish my work, thoughts and projects while they still feel fresh. I have many hobbies and throw myself whole-heartedly from one passion to another. I value humor, self-expression, and genuine connection with people around me. Since life can often be unpredictable, I choose to meet it with satire and creative playfulness.

1. Soviet Theme

Explore the full project→

Soviet-Themed Political Jokes

I built a series of satirical videos on Soviet-era political jokes. With 3D-printed props, live-actor performance and AI-assisted editing, I used dark humor to examine power dynamics. After these videos were removed from China's domestic platforms, I reflected on satire's limits and potential as a tool for historical education.

Documentary: Finding China's Utopia

In this road-trip documentary, I tried to trace how 20th-century Soviet-born utopian ideals travelled and were locally reinterpreted within China. Through visits and field observation, I investigated the complicated legacy of collectivist social experiments. Official Selection, All-American High School Film Festival 2026.

See more→

Semi-Documentary: Becoming Stalin

Inspired by the film Look Who's Back (Hitler returns), I imagined Stalin's home-coming to modern-day Georgia. Combining original screenwriting, on-site filming and re-enactment, I explored divided collective memory of Stalin.

See more→
Photo 1: images/becoming-stalin.png

Academic Research: Workers' Theatre in Early Soviet Russia

In my essay on Soviet agit-prop theatre, I analyzed how state-sponsored performance functioned both as state propaganda and a carrier of public critique. I studied the tension between top-down cultural governance and grassroots popular expression.

Gaming in History

Rejecting textbook-style rote history learning, I built an interactive strategy game simulating 19th-century industrialization and imperialism. I balance historical truth with playable mechanics to let players experience the real-world economic logic behind imperial expansion.

Acting Experiences

My years of stage performance ranged from student musicals, immersive haunted-house theatre to solo dramatic roles. I portrayed Patrick Bateman in my first full solo musical piece and completed professional actor training at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama summer school. Performance skills later informed my documentary re-enactment work.

As an enthusiast of ancient Greek theatre, I reconstructed Homeric myth-worlds inside Minecraft. Building Troy and the Trojan Horse, I created interpretive short videos inspired by EPIC: The Musical, making ancient epic stories accessible for young Chinese-speaking audiences.
